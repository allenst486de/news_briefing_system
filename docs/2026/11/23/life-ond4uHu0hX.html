Cassette is a discombobulating brew of the seemingly casual with the actually deadly bloody serious. Yes, dress up fancy, if you wish, but you can also slide in here in jeans and knitwear for a casual midweek vol-au-vent. No one will judge you. Service will at first come at you brasserie-style – casual, no stress – but can very quickly turn nerdy, obsessive and wallowing in ingredients, provenance and technique. After that, he put in an appearance at Planque, everyone’s favourite wine fiend’s paradise in Haggerston, east London. Now he is here in Covent Garden, lavishing all of that pedigree on quiche, creme brulee and a Saint-Marcellin cheese course – yes, indeed, the food of 1970s hostess trolleys. I’ll say this quietly, to avoid a rumpus and so that you can book yourself a table quickly: I think he’s one of the best chefs in London right now.
