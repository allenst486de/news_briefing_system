42세에 읽기 연령이 4세 수준이었던 남성이 코로나 봉쇄 기간 중 아내의 도움으로 글을 배우기 시작했다. 트럭 운전사였던 그는 우유 구매조차 어려워했고, 아내가 홈스쿨링하는 아들을 도울 수 없다는 수치심이 계기가 됐다. 부분 청각 장애로 학교 적응에 어려움을 겪은 그는 결국 학습 기관의 도움으로 읽고 쓰기를 시작했다.

A moment that changed me: I learned to read and write – at 42

I had worked for 25 years, married and started a family. But a crisis at the supermarket reminded me there was something missing from my lifeIn 2021, at 42 years old, I had the reading age of a four-year-old. I had spent my life up to that point not being able to read and write, navigating the world with careful planning and the help of my wife. During the Covid lockdowns, however, the world turned upside down and my usual way of going through life exploded. My wife was home-schooling our 10-year-old son and I was ashamed to not be able to help; my usual work as a lorry driver was put on hold, and even simple tasks like shopping had become incredibly difficult.I became overwhelmed one day in the supermarket aisles trying to find a milk that my wife and son could drink, since they are both allergic to dairy and soy.
