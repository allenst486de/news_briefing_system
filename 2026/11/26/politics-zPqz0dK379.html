Kishanth Peramalya was denied entry due to sexual assault convictions and sent to Africa where he had no connectionsThe UK sent a Sri Lankan asylum seeker who had been convicted of sexual assault to Eswatini, a southern African country to which he had no connection and where he killed himself.Kishanth Peramalya was part of a group that set sail from India hoping to reach Canada and claim asylum there. The boat was rescued by the Royal Navy in October 2021 when it got into difficulty and brought to Diego Garcia in the remote British Indian Ocean Territory, which hosts a US-UK military base.
