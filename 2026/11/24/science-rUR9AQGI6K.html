Emotions play a significant part in physical symptoms – but not in the way people often imagineWhen Diego started seeing bright flashing lights in his field of vision and felt his legs go weak, he thought he knew immediately what was happening. We are accustomed to thinking about physical symptoms as being caused by physical events; viruses, inflammation, bleeding.
