My mum would rustle up hers in the time it took the pasta to cook, which gave it a crunchiness that sometimes made me cry (I swear to God, those onions were practically raw). And my dad would let his bubble away for the duration of Gladiators, meaning that by the time it was dished up at the start of Blind Date, it had an intensity that felt positively foreign.I’m not alone in being a big fan of spag bol – the dish is often voted the nation’s favourite, and there’s now even a National Spaghetti Bolognese Day (22 May) – but I do sometimes wonder what it is about this relatively simple import, essentially a combo of mince and tomatoes plonked upon pasta, that has won over millions and withstood the vagaries of time.
